Privacy Policy for Rentzu

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Rentzu ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Rentzu mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — email address and login credentials when you create an account.
  • Location data — approximate or precise location to help you search for and view nearby rental properties.
  • Photos and camera access — used when you upload property photos or documents.
  • Payment and subscription information — subscription tier, purchase history, and transaction identifiers processed via Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not store your full payment card details.
  • Usage analytics and crash logs — app performance data and diagnostic logs used to fix bugs and improve the App.
  • Push notification tokens — device tokens used to deliver notifications about your listings, messages, or account activity.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, and advertising identifiers.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App and its rental/property features.
  • To process subscriptions and manage your billing tier (Normal / Pro, monthly / yearly).
  • To send you service-related and account notifications.
  • To monitor and analyze usage in order to improve reliability and features.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data with service providers who help us operate the App, including:

  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat — for processing and managing subscriptions.
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers — for diagnosing and improving app stability.
  • Cloud hosting and push-notification providers — to operate core app infrastructure.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can access, update, or delete your account information from within the App or by contacting us.
  • You can disable location, camera, or notification permissions at any time from your device settings.
  • You can request a copy of, or deletion of, your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Rentzu is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
